# ShotDesk website product brief

## Purpose

Explain ShotDesk's local screenshot-processing workflow and direct visitors to the source and preview releases.

## Audience and action

Technical users who accumulate screenshots—especially developers, designers, support teams, and students. Visitors should understand the local pipeline and then inspect the source or preview releases.

## Product truth

- ShotDesk is a local-first desktop application targeting Windows and Linux.
- It indexes selected folders and supports searching filenames, paths, OCR text, and dominant-color aliases.
- Image dimensions, available EXIF details, embedded DPI, and dominant colors are processed locally. GPS coordinates are not stored.
- OCR models are downloaded only after the user enables text search. After setup, OCR runs locally; screenshots are never uploaded.
- Windows x64 has been verified. Linux is configured but still needs build and installation validation on a Linux host.
- Collections group indexed captures without moving originals.

## Language and tracking

English (`en-US`) is the default, with French (`fr-FR`) available. The site includes no analytics, tracking cookies, or third-party hosted fonts.
